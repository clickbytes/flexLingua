# A1 structural coverage

`npm run coverage:a1` reports declared lexemes, CORE senses, morphology bindings, grammar frames, and the project-authored A1 pilot. `npm run check:coverage` verifies inventory references and coverage invariants.

Structural coverage describes what is declared in repository data. It does not measure language proficiency. `cefr.percentage` remains null without a representative, legally sourced denominator and independently assessed sentence-level tests for both Polish-to-English and English-to-Polish.

## Evidence

- The frozen target inventory is `data/coverage/a1-targets.json`: 16 concepts plus 7 constructions. The separate EN→PL family registry is `data/coverage/a1-enpl-constructions.json`; it does not expand G2.
- `required` is the frozen target status; `implemented` is the declared construction status; `exercised` means a family points to a real fixture case; `verified` means its referenced suite passed; `known-gap` records a missing contract or evidence. The ledger reports declared and executed results separately, by EN→PL/PL→EN direction and positive/refusal kind. Planned families have an explicit gap and are not counted as failures or successes.
- A1-ENPL-01 pins two positive directions and two refusal cases in `tests/fixtures/gold-a1-enpl-basics.json`, including “This is a simple book.” and its static CORE expectation.
- A1-ENPL-10 adds eight positive classification cases (four per direction) and two explicit refusals for unsupported past and WH questions in the same fixture. Present negation and yes/no questions preserve the `classification` CORE frame, separate from identity.
- A1-ENPL-08 pins six positive bilingual cases and four explicit refusals in `tests/fixtures/gold-a1-third-person.json`. Third-person pronouns preserve a typed unresolved referent and resolve in discourse only to one compatible person antecedent; the CORE never substitutes `person.girl` or `person.boy` for a pronoun. Its progressive case reuses the existing imperfective contract; Polish present realizes to English simple present, whose reverse reading may carry `aspect: unknown`.
- The original sentence corpus is described in [G2](g2-a1-gold.md).
- STATE-05 adds 48 hand-authored PL↔EN predication and possession examples in `tests/fixtures/gold-state-a1.json` (24 per direction), including present-tense negation and yes/no questions, six CORE round trips across identity, predication, and possession, and 7 explicit refusals for unsupported identity, malformed agreement, and Polish past state realization. The cases are regression evidence for the declared subset, not a representative language corpus.
- A1-ENPL-03 and A1-ENPL-11 share `tests/fixtures/gold-a1-bare-beverages.json`: twelve project-authored EN↔PL examples cover five mass-noun beverage senses and both `drink` and `visual_perception`. Four refusals preserve count-noun articles, reject the unmodelled portion reading of “a coffee”, and enforce semantic role types. These samples are internal regression evidence, not an independent benchmark or CEFR evidence.
- A1-ENPL-04 adds four project-authored EN↔PL locative examples in `tests/fixtures/gold-a1-existential-locatives.json`, preserving typed CORE `location` and number agreement, with explicit refusals for unsupported tense, polarity, questions, missing locations, and prepositions. These samples are internal regression evidence, not an independent benchmark or CEFR evidence.
- A1-ENPL-05 adds ten project-authored EN↔PL past state predication examples in `tests/fixtures/gold-a1-past-state-predication.json`, covering five Polish gender/number agreement classes, with explicit refusals for past negation/questions, malformed agreement, and other state kinds. These samples are internal regression evidence, not an independent benchmark or CEFR evidence.
- A1-ENPL-06/09 records 30 project-authored EN↔PL present-progressive cases in `tests/fixtures/gold-a1-present-progressive.json` for six configured verbs: six positive, six negative, and six yes/no EN→PL cases; six positive and six negative PL→EN cases retain simple English present. The contract preserves imperfective CORE, uses Polish negation and `czy`, and refuses unsupported tense, agreement, and verbs. Polish present maps to English simple present without inferring progressive aspect. Five explicit refusals and these samples are internal regression evidence, not an independent benchmark or CEFR evidence.
- The combined report and its limits are documented in [G3](g3-a1-report.md).
- The pilot data in `data/coverage/a1-pilot.json` is authored for this project and is not copied from Oxford.

Run `npm run report:a1` to inspect the frozen target inventory and `npm run report:a1-evidence` for a catalog-only ledger (`not-run`). Run `npm run check:a1-gold` for G2 and `npm run check:a1-evidence` to execute the internal suites, including STATE-05 and A1-ENPL-01. These fixed samples are not an independent benchmark. Report tested constructions, directions, and refusals; do not present these checks as CEFR certification.
